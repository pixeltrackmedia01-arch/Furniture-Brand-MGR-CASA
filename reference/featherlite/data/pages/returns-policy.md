**For all products.** Once we have delivered the product and left the customer’s premises, we will NOT be able to accommodate any change requests  (other than  damage or defective request). We request the customer or customer’s family member to be present during delivery to ensure that they check the furniture at the time of delivery.

We, at **Featherlite Collections**, We **do not offer exchanges** unless the product is damaged or defective on arrival.The exchanged product will be the same as the product purchased by the customer.

**Disclaimer****:** Product Images shown on website is only for representation purpose few features may vary as per original product.

**Cancellation****:**Once booked, cancellation is allowed only within  24 hours of placing the order (there would be no charges applicable) after which the order will be shipped. No cancellations are allowed for products bought under discount.

If the customer wishes to cancel the order post 24 hours, the customer must refuse delivery of the product. Once the refusal of the delivery has been confirmed, a refund will be initiated towards the product.

If a customer receives a damaged or defective product, the customer must send an email to [customercare@featherlitefurniture.com](mailto:customercare@featherlitefurniture.com) with images of the product within 24 hours. Once our quality team confirms on the issue, a replacement of that defective part will be initiated.

If the customer wishes for a refund for any other reason the customer must bear a fee of Rs 1500 for sanitizing, re-packing, quality checks and transportation for the same. Please note the return of the product in this case should be informed to the **customer care** within 48 hrs of delivery of the product

## Returns

1. We accept returns only in case of product damages on arrival
2. You should inform us of the receipt of a Damaged/ Defective product maximum within 24 hours of delivery.
3. Also take photograph of the damaged product & send mail to our customer care mail id [customercare@featherlitefurniture.com](mailto:support@featherlitefurniture.com)
4. Refund will be provided only in case a replacement or replacement part for the same product is not available. If a particular part is faulty then the that part shall be arranged and replaced.
5. The rectification of the damaged products during warranty will take around 7 Days from the time receipt of complaint and replacement will take up to 15 days from the receipt of complaint.
6. If we are not able the rectify the issue or replace spare part or replace the product then the refund will be processed in 7 days.

## Who pays return shipping on a damaged product

We have reverse pick up and replacement facility at our cost from your place in case of damaged product.

## Cancellations

### By the Company

If the supplied product is found defective or for any other technical issue the product cannot be repaired or replaced, the option will be given to buy similar product .The difference amount has to be borne by the customer or will be refunded in 6-7 business days

## Refunds

1. Refund method shall be via cheque or NEFT transfer or transfer to the Credit Card/Debit Card from where the Payment had been made. The choice of the same shall lie on Featherlite. Featherlite shall notify the Customer of such a transfer through email and/or through phone.
2. Please follow up with your respective bank/ card issuer post completion of refund process by Featherlite, to check on credit of amount in your account.
3. The refund will take 2 weeks.
4. In case of refunds made through cheques the same will be sent by courier within 7 working days . However you need to consider transit time for for cheque reaching to your place.

* You should inform us of the receipt of a Damaged/ Defective product maximum within 24 hours of delivery.
* Products returned without intimation to Featherlite as mentioned above will  NOT be accepted and the same will be sent back  to the customer  and will not be processed for refund. The cost incurred to send such materials back will have to be borne by the customer
