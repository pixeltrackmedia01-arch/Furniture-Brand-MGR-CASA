# Office Chair Buying Guide: How to Choose

Source: https://featherlitestore.com/pages/office-chair-buying-guide

Start with the measurements that affect fit: seat height, seat depth and the space under and around your desk. Then compare the controls you want to use, including lumbar adjustment, recline, armrests and a headrest.

## 1. Measure the seat and your workspace

* **Seat height:** Check the chair’s minimum and maximum seat height. If you can try the chair, set it where your feet rest flat and check how that position lines up with your desk.
* **Seat width and depth:** Look for enough room at the sides and a gap behind your knees when your back rests against the chair. A seat slider lets you adjust the depth on models that include it.
* **Desk clearance:** Measure the gap under the desk for the armrests and the floor space around it for the chair and castors. Compare overall chair width and depth as well as seat size.

For example, the [Comet without Headrest](/products/featherlite-comet-medium-back-mesh-chair-with-adjustable-arms) lists a seat height of 435 ± 10 mm to 535 ± 10 mm and a seat depth of 455 ± 10 mm. The [Optima with Headrest](/products/featherlite-optima-high-back-mesh-chair-with-multilock-seat-sliding-mechanism-three-way-adjustable-arms) lists a seat height of 57.5 to 62 cm, a seat depth of 51 cm and a seat slider. Use those figures to compare fit with your desk.

## 2. Compare the recline and lumbar controls

A synchro mechanism links movement of the seat and back. Check the exact model for its recline positions and resistance controls. The [Liberate with Headrest](/products/featherlite-liberate-high-back-mesh-chair-with-dynaflex-back-support-to-provide-dual-frame-flexibility-multilock-mechanism) offers multiple recline positions and a DynaFlex twin frame. The [Amaze with Headrest (Black)](/products/featherlite-amaze-high-back-mesh-chair-with-self-calibrating-multilock-mechanism-fabric-upholstered-cushion-seatblack) has a self-calibrating multilock mechanism that adjusts resistance to the user’s weight.

Look at how the lumbar support moves and where it meets your back. The [Contact Medium Back (Black)](/products/featherlite-contact-medium-back-mesh-chair-with-single-lock-synchro-mechanism-adjustable-lumbar-supportblack) has integrated adjustable lumbar support. The Optima with Headrest lists lumbar height and depth controls.

For exact seat and backrest measurements, [compare the retail Astro, Versa and Anatom chairs with and without headrests](/pages/compare#astro-versa-anatom). Astro and Versa use single-lock synchro recline, while Anatom uses multilock recline.

## 3. Check the armrests, headrest and materials

* **Armrests:** Compare the adjustment directions. Amaze with Headrest (Black) has height-adjustable arms. On the Optima with Headrest, the arms adjust in height, front to back and side to side.
* **Headrest:** If you want head support while reclining, check the headrest position on the exact model. If you prefer an open upper back, compare models without a headrest by their published backrest height and seat dimensions.
* **Back and seat materials:** Compare the construction listed on the product page and try the materials if you can. Comet without Headrest, for example, lists an AN 70 mesh back and a fabric-upholstered cushion seat.
* **Castors:** Check the wheel material and ask Featherlite about compatibility with your floor if it is unclear. Comet without Headrest lists nylon castors.

## 4. Compare current prices and check the warranty

Set a budget after you’ve narrowed the list by fit and adjustment controls. [See current prices and availability for eight Featherlite chairs](/pages/compare#current-price) and the models currently under ₹10,000. Check the exact product page before you order.

Check the warranty by component. The models on the comparison page list three years of mechanism coverage. For other components, exclusions and conditions, check the exact product page and the [Featherlite warranty terms](/pages/warranty).
