# Office Chairs for Back Pain: What Actually Helps

Source: https://featherlitestore.com/pages/office-chairs-for-back-pain

If your lower back hurts at the end of every workday, you do not need a better mattress. You need a better chair. Here is what actually relieves back pain, backed by what physiotherapists recommend in India, and what is available without taking out a loan.

## Why most office chairs cause back pain

The standard office chair was designed assuming you sit upright at a 90° angle, hands on the keyboard, feet flat on the floor. **Nobody sits like this.** Most people lean back 10-30 degrees, hunch forward when reading screens, and slide their hips forward by mid-afternoon. Cheap chairs ignore all three of these realities.

The fix is not a fancier chair. It is a chair with four specific features that adapt to how you actually sit.

## The four features that relieve lower back pain

### 1. Adjustable lumbar support

The lower spine has a natural inward curve at the L3-L5 vertebrae. Your chair needs to support that curve, not a generic "lumbar zone" the manufacturer designed for a 5'8" male. Look for a chair where the lumbar pad slides up and down independently, so you can position it exactly at the small of your back.

### 2. Synchro tilt or multilock mechanism

Sitting in one position locks muscles. The chair should let you lean back without losing lumbar contact. A **synchro tilt** mechanism reclines the back faster than the seat (typically 2:1) so the lumbar pad stays glued to your spine. A **multilock** mechanism does this AND locks at any angle so you can hold a comfortable lean.

### 3. Seat depth adjustment (slider)

If the front of the seat cuts into the back of your knees, you will scoot your hips forward. That destroys your lumbar contact. A seat slider lets you adjust depth by about 5cm, enough to fit most body proportions.

### 4. A cushion seat, not flat mesh

Mesh seats look ergonomic but they distribute weight onto your sit bones (ischial tuberosities). Over hours, that bruises soft tissue and shifts your spine. A cushioned, fabric-upholstered seat distributes pressure more evenly.

## What the chair alone cannot fix

Even the best chair will not undo eight hours of sitting. Pair the chair with: a monitor at eye level so you stop hunching, a five-minute stretch every hour, and, if the budget allows, a height-adjustable desk so you can stand for part of the day.

## Talk to a physio before spending ₹30,000
