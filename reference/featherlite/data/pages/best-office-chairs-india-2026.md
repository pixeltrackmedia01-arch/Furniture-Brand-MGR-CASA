## Questions to answer before you choose

What should I compare first when choosing a Featherlite office chair?

Start with seat width, seat depth and the seat-height range. Then compare the lumbar, arm and recline adjustments you want, the seat material, and the current price. Open the exact product page to check its full measurements before you choose.

Which shortlisted chairs have lumbar support that adjusts in height?

The two Black Amaze versions, Astro with Headrest, Versa without Headrest and Opus without Headrest each document lumbar support that adjusts in height independently of the seat back. Astro’s support is padded; Opus uses an asynchronous lumbar design. Compare the exact versions in the table above.

How do the single-lock synchro and self-calibrating multilock options differ?

Astro and Versa have a single-lock synchro mechanism and a tension knob for adjusting the force needed to recline. Amaze and Opus use self-calibrating multilock mechanisms that adapt recline resistance to your weight. Compare lumbar, arm and seat adjustments separately.

Which of these chairs has a mesh seat as well as a mesh back?

The Grey Pinnacle has a mesh seat and back. The other versions compared here pair mesh backs with cushion or fabric-upholstered seats, as specified in the table. Check the exact product version when choosing your preferred seat material.

What should I compare when choosing a version with a headrest?

Choose a version sold with a headrest, then check its listed adjustments and whole-chair measurements. The Black Amaze versions with and without a headrest are separate products. Their seat dimensions match, while their overall chair heights differ; compare both product pages before ordering.

Which of these chairs currently cost less than ₹10,000?

Use the [current under ₹10,000 section](#under-10000) to see whether any shortlisted versions are available below that budget. Check the exact version’s current price and availability before you order.

What should I do if a shortlisted chair is sold out?

Check the exact colour and headrest version you want. If it is unavailable, [browse the office-chair range](/collections/office-chairs) and compare the features and measurements that matter to you. A different model or colour can have different specifications.
