## Astro vs Versa vs Anatom

Astro and Versa both use single-lock synchro recline. Astro adds padded lumbar support and has a lower listed seat-height range. Versa has the widest and deepest seat of these three chair families. Anatom shares Astro’s listed seat-height range and uses multilock recline.

* **Astro.** Padded lumbar support that adjusts in height independently of the backrest. Single-lock synchro recline with a tension knob. Seat height 435 ± 10 mm to 535 ± 10 mm. Seat 485 ± 10 mm wide and 450 ± 10 mm deep.
  + [Astro without Headrest (Black)](/products/featherlite-astro-medium-back-mesh-chair-with-padded-lumbar-support-cushion-seatblack?variant=43907041951803). Backrest 520 ± 10 mm above the seat. Net chair weight 14.5 ± 0.5 kg.
  + [Astro with Headrest (Black)](/products/featherlite-astro-high-back-mesh-chair-with-padded-lumbar-support-cushion-seatblack?variant=43907069050939). Backrest 520 ± 10 mm above the seat. Net chair weight 15.4 ± 0.5 kg.
* **Versa.** Lumbar support that adjusts in height independently of the backrest. Single-lock synchro recline with a tension knob. Seat height 465 ± 10 mm to 565 ± 10 mm. Seat 505 ± 10 mm wide and 465 ± 10 mm deep.
  + [Versa without Headrest](/products/featherlite-versa-medium-back-mesh-chair-with-single-lock-synchro-mechanism-fabric-upholstered-seat?variant=43907041689659). Backrest 500 ± 10 mm above the seat. Net chair weight 13 ± 0.5 kg.
  + [Versa with Headrest](/products/featherlite-versa-high-back-mesh-chair-with-single-lock-synchro-mechanism-fabric-upholstered-seat?variant=43907054272571). Backrest 570 ± 10 mm above the seat. Net chair weight 13.6 ± 0.5 kg.
* **Anatom.** Lumbar support that adjusts in height independently of the backrest. Multilock recline with a tension knob. Seat height 435 ± 10 mm to 535 ± 10 mm. Seat 480 ± 10 mm wide and 440 ± 10 mm deep.
  + [Anatom without Headrest (Black)](/products/featherlite-anatom-medium-back-mesh-chair-with-multilock-mechanism-fabric-upholstered-cushion-seatblack?variant=43907054338107). Backrest 545 ± 10 mm above the seat. Net chair weight 15.8 ± 0.5 kg.
  + [Anatom with Headrest (Black)](/products/featherlite-anatom-high-back-mesh-chair-with-multilock-mechanism-fabric-upholstered-cushion-seatblack?variant=43907070099515). Backrest 645 ± 10 mm above the seat. Net chair weight 15.8 ± 0.5 kg.

These measurements are for the linked Featherlite Store versions. Backrest height is measured from the seat. Net chair weight is the weight of the chair itself.

## How to compare these chairs

* **Seat and desk fit:** Compare the seat height range with your desk setup and the seat width and depth with how you sit. Use overall chair width and depth to check floor space.
* **Headrest:** Choose whether you want head support when reclining. Check the listed backrest height from the seat and how the headrest meets you when you try the chair.
* **Recline and lumbar controls:** Liberate with Headrest lists a DynaFlex twin frame and multiple recline positions. Astro with Headrest (Black) lists padded adjustable lumbar. Contact Medium Back (Black) lists integrated adjustable lumbar.
* **Seat and arm adjustments:** Optima with Headrest lists a seat slider and armrests that adjust in height, front to back and side to side. Compare those controls with the height-adjustable arms listed for Amaze with Headrest (Black).
* **Warranty:** Each of these eight product pages lists three years on the mechanism. Check the exact product page and the [Featherlite warranty terms](/pages/warranty) for other components, conditions and exclusions.
