# Shipping policy

Source: https://featherlitestore.com/policies/shipping-policy

Free shipping across India on every Featherlite order.

## Delivery times

Orders are delivered in 3 to 5 working days.

## Delivery area

We deliver across India.

## Shipping costs

All orders ship for free across India. There are no hidden charges or delivery fees.

## What you receive

Products are shipped in a knocked-down form (flat-pack) with assembly instructions and all required tools. Most products take 15-30 minutes to assemble. For installation by a Featherlite expert, please use the "Installation by expert" link on the product page (additional charges apply).

## Tracking

All orders are drop shipped, so tracking information is not available. For an update on your order, contact customer care at customercare@featherlitefurniture.com or 080 4719 1010.

## Failed delivery / wrong address

If a delivery fails because the address is incorrect or no one is available to receive the package, please contact us within 48 hours so we can re-arrange. Re-delivery is free for the first attempt; additional attempts may incur charges.
